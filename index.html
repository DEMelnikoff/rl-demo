Two-Step Task

Interactive computational RL demo — Model-Free · Model-Based · Successor Representation

Task Structure & Algorithms

Model-Free (Q-learning)

Updates Q(s,a) via TD errors. Values propagate slowly through experience. Insensitive to both outcome and transition revaluation until re-experienced.

Model-Based RL

Maintains explicit T(s,a,s') and R(s,a) models; plans via value iteration after each trial. Immediately sensitive to both revaluations.

Successor Representation

M(s,s') encodes expected discounted future occupancy; V = M·w. Quick to adapt to outcome revaluation (update w), slow for transition revaluation (M must re-learn).
